Help & Support

Need assistance with the Parkoo app, parking sessions, digital receipts, payments, or your account? Find guidance below or email our support team directly.

Access: Public (No login required) Coverage: UK Parking Application Support Email: dev@swithasystems.com

Contact Parkoo Support

Our support team assists via email with account access, parking sessions, digital receipts, payment queries, and privacy or account deletion requests.

  • Support & Privacy Email: dev@swithasystems.com
  • Support Channel: Email support only (no telephone support)

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include the email or phone number linked to your account, your vehicle registration (if relevant), the parking location and date, and any digital receipt reference.

Account & Login Assistance

Having trouble signing in, updating your profile, or verifying your contact details?

  • Ensure you are using the latest version of the Parkoo app from the Apple App Store or Google Play.
  • Check that your internet connection is active and that any verification email or code has not gone to spam or junk folders.
  • If you changed your email or phone number and cannot sign in, email dev@swithasystems.com with your previous account details.

Parking Booking & Session Assistance

Help with finding participating locations, checking tariffs, or managing an active session:

  • Always verify the parking zone or location code, tariff, restrictions, and vehicle registration number before confirming a session.
  • Confirm your session shows as active inside the app before leaving your vehicle.
  • If a car park experiences connectivity issues or an error prevents starting a session, follow on-site payment signage to avoid a parking charge.

Payment & Refund Assistance

Questions about a charge, failed payment, or digital receipt?

  • Check your in-app session history and digital receipts for the transaction reference and tariff breakdown.
  • If you notice a duplicate charge or billing issue, email dev@swithasystems.com with the transaction date, amount, and parking location.
  • Review our Cancellation & Refund Policy for details on how operator tariff rules apply.

Account Deletion Assistance

Want to permanently close your Parkoo account and delete your personal data?

  • You can initiate account deletion directly inside the Parkoo mobile app.
  • Read our step-by-step instructions and data retention details on the Account & Data Deletion page.
  • If you cannot access your account, email dev@swithasystems.com with subject Account Deletion Request.

Privacy-Related Enquiries

For questions about how Parkoo collects, uses, stores, or shares personal information—or to exercise your UK GDPR rights (access, correction, erasure, restriction, or objection)—please review our Privacy Policy and email dev@swithasystems.com.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Parkoo and where can I use it?

Parkoo is a UK-focused parking application designed to let you find participating on-street parking and car parks, view tariffs and restrictions up front, start, extend, and stop parking sessions, and pay in one place using open standards and the National Parking Platform (NPP) (subject to membership approval and technical onboarding).

Where can I find my parking confirmation and digital receipt?

Active session confirmations and digital receipts are available inside the Parkoo app once your parking payment has been processed. If you need help retrieving a receipt, email dev@swithasystems.com with your account details and parking date.

Does uninstalling the Parkoo app delete my account?

No. Uninstalling the app only removes it from your device. To permanently delete your account and personal data, use the in-app Delete Account option or follow the instructions on our Account & Data Deletion page.

What should I do if I receive a Parking Charge Notice or Penalty Charge Notice (PCN)?

Car parks and on-street zones are managed and enforced by the relevant local authority or private parking operator. Follow the appeals instructions printed on the notice and keep a copy of your Parkoo digital receipt as proof of payment. Email dev@swithasystems.com if you need help retrieving a receipt record.

How do I control location or notification permissions?

You can grant, limit, or revoke location and push notification permissions at any time through your iPhone/iPad (iOS Settings) or Android device settings.
