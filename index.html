About Parkoo

Parkoo is a UK-focused parking app built to simplify how people park and pay. Instead of juggling multiple operator apps, Parkoo connects you to a growing network of participating car parks through one platform — using the National Parking Platform (NPP) as the backbone.

Our initial focus is to help motorists:
• Discover participating parking locations
• View tariffs and restrictions
• Start, extend, and stop parking sessions
• Pay seamlessly via one app

We’re committed to expanding into additional parking services over time, all on a standards-based, interoperable platform.
