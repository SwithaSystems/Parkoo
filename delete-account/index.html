Account & Data Deletion

You can request the permanent deletion of your Parkoo account and associated personal information at any time.

Access: Public (No login required) Support Email: dev@swithasystems.com

How to Request Account Deletion

To delete your Parkoo account and personal data, please send an email request to our support team:

Email: dev@swithasystems.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request

Please include the following details in your email:

  • Account Identifier: The email address or mobile phone number registered to your Parkoo account.
  • Vehicle Registration: Any vehicle registration mark (VRM) saved in your account (if applicable).
  • Reason for Deletion: The reason you wish to delete your account (optional, but helps us improve the service).
  • Confirmation Statement: Explicit statement confirming that you request permanent deletion of your Parkoo account and personal data.

For your security and privacy, our support team may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the deletion request.

Before Submitting Your Request

  • Active Sessions: Make sure you have ended any active parking sessions so that final parking charges settle cleanly.
  • Digital Receipts: Save or download any receipts you need for personal records, tax, or business expenses prior to deletion.
  • App Uninstall: Please note that uninstalling or removing the Parkoo mobile app from your device does not delete your account or server-side data. You must reach out via email to request account deletion.

What Happens When Your Account Is Deleted

Once your deletion request has been verified and processed:

  • Your personal profile details (such as name, email address, phone number, and vehicle details) are permanently removed or irreversibly anonymised.
  • Your login credentials and active device authentication sessions are terminated immediately.
  • You will no longer receive account-related communications or push notifications from Parkoo.

Information Retained for Legal & Financial Compliance

Certain minimal transaction and accounting records (such as completed payment receipts, transaction timestamps, and refund records) cannot be deleted immediately and will be retained strictly in accordance with statutory UK financial, tax, and accounting retention laws. These records are stored securely, restricted from active profile use, and purged once the statutory retention period expires.

Need Further Assistance?

If you have any questions regarding your account or data deletion, please contact us at dev@swithasystems.com or visit our Help & Support page.
