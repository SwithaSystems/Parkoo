Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your Parkoo account directly inside the Parkoo mobile application. This page explains how to initiate deletion, what happens to your personal information, and how to contact support if you cannot access your account.

Access: Public (No login required) Support Email: dev@swithasystems.com

1. How to Delete Your Account from the Mobile Application

Parkoo supports in-app account deletion so you can permanently close your account directly from your mobile device:

  1. Open the Parkoo Mobile App

    Sign in to the Parkoo iOS or Android application using the account you wish to delete.

  2. Open Account Settings

    Navigate to your Account or Profile settings area inside the app.

  3. Select “Delete Account”

    Review the account deletion details, ensure no active parking session is running, and choose to confirm deletion.

  4. Confirm Deletion

    Complete any verification prompt if requested. Your deletion request is submitted to Parkoo's backend service and your session will be signed out.

2. Before You Delete Your Account

  • End Active Parking Sessions: Stop any ongoing parking session before deleting your account so charges settle properly and you do not lose visibility of an active session.
  • Save Digital Receipts: Download or save any parking receipts needed for expenses, VAT, or operator queries prior to deleting your account.
  • Uninstalling Is Not Deletion: Removing the Parkoo app from your phone does not delete your account or server-side data. You must complete deletion inside the app or request deletion via email support.

3. What Happens to Your Personal Information

Once your account deletion request is processed by Parkoo:

Data Category Action on Account Deletion
Account profile (name, email, phone number) Permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymised so it can no longer identify you.
Saved vehicles & app preferences Removed from your active account profile.
Authentication sessions & push tokens Revoked so you are signed out and will no longer receive account push notifications.

4. Information Retained for Legal or Financial Reasons

Certain limited records cannot be deleted immediately where Parkoo is required by law or legitimate compliance obligations to retain them:

  • Financial & Tax Records: Completed parking payment records, digital receipts, refund logs, and chargeback records required for statutory UK accounting, tax, and audit obligations.
  • Operator Reconciliation & Disputes: Minimal transaction references needed to settle completed parking sessions with participating car park operators or resolve active payment disputes.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: Minimal records strictly required by applicable UK law or to prevent abuse and fraud.

5. Cannot Access Your Account? Contact Support

If you cannot sign in to the Parkoo mobile app (for example, you lost access to your registered email or phone number, or ran into an error while deleting your account), you can request account deletion assistance without logging in by emailing Parkoo Support.

Email Support for Account Deletion: dev@swithasystems.com (Email support only)

Please include in your email:

  • The email address or phone number registered to your Parkoo account.
  • Subject line: Account Deletion Request.
  • Confirmation that you want your Parkoo account and personal data permanently deleted (we may ask you to verify account ownership before processing).
