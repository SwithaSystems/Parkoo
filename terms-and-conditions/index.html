2. Parking Search and Sessions

Parkoo enables motorists to discover participating on-street parking zones and car parks across the UK, view tariffs and parking restrictions, and start, extend, and stop parking sessions from one application powered by open standards and the National Parking Platform (NPP) (subject to membership approval and technical onboarding).

Any session extensions or reservations are subject to the maximum stay limits, operating hours, and availability rules displayed in the app for the selected location.

3. Parking Availability and Pricing

Parkoo displays parking charges, tariffs, and restriction notices up front before you confirm a parking session, based on data supplied by participating operators and connected open parking feeds.

  • Parking tariffs, hours of operation, and bay restrictions are determined by the relevant parking operator or local authority and may change without prior notice.
  • Any displayed availability indicators are provided for convenience and do not guarantee a physical parking space will be free upon arrival unless explicitly confirmed as a reserved bay.
  • All charges and applicable fees are shown in the app prior to payment confirmation.

4. Booking Confirmation

A parking session is valid only after payment authorisation has succeeded and an active confirmation and digital receipt are displayed in the Parkoo application.

  • Before leaving your vehicle, verify in the app that the parking location/zone, vehicle registration mark, and session expiry time are accurate.
  • If payment fails or confirmation is not received due to a network issue, your vehicle is not covered and you must retry or use an alternative on-site payment method.

5. Cancellation and Refund Policies

Parking sessions and tariffs are governed by the rules of the participating parking operator or local authority for the selected site:

  • Active Pay-As-You-Park Sessions: Where supported by the location, stopping an active session ends further time charges according to the tariff rules shown in the app; time already elapsed or minimum stay tariffs are charged as displayed before confirmation.
  • Duplicate or Erroneous Charges: If you believe you were charged incorrectly or twice for the same session, please contact dev@swithasystems.com with your digital receipt details so we can investigate with the relevant payment provider and parking operator.
  • Operator-Specific Rules: Any additional cancellation windows or non-refundable tariff conditions shown at checkout apply to that booking.

6. Payment Processing

Payments are processed securely through integrated third-party payment providers. By confirming a parking session, you authorise the charge for the displayed tariff and any disclosed applicable fees.

Digital receipts are made available for completed transactions. If a payment is declined, reversed, or disputed, Parkoo may suspend unpaid session access until the balance is settled.
