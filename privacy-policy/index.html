1. Who We Are

Parkoo is a UK-focused parking application built to simplify how motorists find, start, extend, stop, and pay for parking across participating on-street zones and car parks using open standards and the National Parking Platform (NPP) (subject to membership approval and technical onboarding).

For privacy enquiries or data protection requests, you can reach us by email at dev@swithasystems.com.

2. Personal Information Collected During Registration

When you register or maintain a Parkoo account, we collect only the information you provide to create your account, verify your identity, manage your parking sessions, and send receipts or service notices. Depending on the fields you complete in the app, this may include:

  • Name, Email Address, and Phone Number: Used to identify your account, authenticate sign-in, communicate about active sessions or support requests, and deliver digital receipts.
  • Vehicle Details: Vehicle registration mark (VRM) or saved vehicle details you add so parking operators can validate your active parking session.
  • Account Preferences: Basic preferences you configure within the application.

3. Location Data and Its Purpose

With your permission, Parkoo uses your device's location services to help you find available on-street parking and car parks near you, display relevant local tariffs and restrictions, and assist you in selecting the correct parking location or zone.

  • Location access is controlled by your iOS or Android system permissions and is used only to support parking discovery and location selection features while using the app.
  • You can enable, restrict, or revoke location permissions at any time in your device settings. If disabled, you can search for parking locations manually where manual search is available.

4. Parking Searches and Booking Information

When you look up tariffs or start, extend, or stop a parking session, Parkoo processes the booking and session details necessary to provide the parking service and issue digital receipts, including:

  • Selected parking location, car park or zone identifier, and operator details.
  • Session start time, duration, extensions, stop time, and booking status.
  • Applicable tariffs, charges paid, and digital receipt records.
  • Vehicle registration associated with the session.

5. Payment Processing and Third-Party Payment Providers

Payments for parking sessions are processed securely through authorised third-party payment service providers integrated with Parkoo. Parkoo does not store raw payment card numbers or card security codes (CVV) directly on your device.

Parkoo retains only the transaction reference, payment status, amount, currency, and receipt metadata needed to confirm your parking session, issue digital receipts, handle refunds or disputes, and satisfy accounting obligations.

6. Device Information and Technical Logs

To keep Parkoo reliable, secure, and compatible across devices, our services may automatically record limited technical and diagnostic data when you use the app or website, such as:

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version.
  • IP address, request timestamps, and server response status for security, rate limiting, and troubleshooting.
  • Error and diagnostic logs needed to investigate bugs or service interruptions.

7. Push Notifications

If you grant notification permissions on your device, Parkoo may send operational push notifications—such as parking session confirmations, expiry reminders, extension updates, or important account notices—using a device push token.

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings without affecting your ability to view active sessions inside the app.

8. Third-Party Integrations and SDKs

To deliver core parking functionality, Parkoo integrates with essential third-party services and software components, including:

  • Parking Ecosystem & Operators: National Parking Platform (NPP) infrastructure and participating parking operators to retrieve tariffs, restrictions, and validate active parking sessions.
  • Mapping & Location Services: Map and geolocation services used to display parking locations.
  • Payment & Notification Providers: Authorised payment gateways and platform push notification services (such as Apple Push Notification service) required to complete payments and deliver session alerts.
  • Infrastructure & Diagnostics: Cloud hosting and technical monitoring tools used strictly to operate and maintain service reliability.

9. Data Storage and Security

Parkoo uses appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. All communication between the Parkoo mobile app, this website, and backend services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

Access to production systems and user records is restricted to authorised personnel and essential service processors on a least-privilege basis.

10. Data Sharing

Parkoo does not sell your personal information. We share information only where necessary to deliver the service or meet legal requirements:

  • Parking Operators & NPP Infrastructure: Session, zone, and vehicle registration details needed to validate your right to park and reconcile charges with participating operators.
  • Service Processors: Payment processors, hosting providers, and notification services acting on our instructions under appropriate confidentiality and data protection terms.
  • Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Law enforcement, regulators, courts, or authorities where disclosure is legally required under applicable UK law.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to operate your account, provide parking services and digital receipts, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and comply with statutory UK legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

When you delete your account, profile and saved personal details are deleted or irreversibly anonymised, except for limited payment and transaction records that must be retained for statutory financial reporting, tax compliance, or dispute resolution.

12. Account and Data Deletion & Your Rights

Under applicable UK data protection law (including UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, as well as rights to restrict or object to certain processing and to data portability where applicable.

You can request the deletion of your Parkoo account and personal data at any time by emailing support. Step-by-step instructions and required email details are available on our Account & Data Deletion page.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you have concerns about how your data is handled.
